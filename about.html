ABOUT ME

From Core Banking Data To The Modern Cloud Stack.

I'm Tanya Amber, a Data Engineer and Analytics Engineer with four years' hands-on experience in SQL-based ETL, data migration and BI reporting for global banking clients, followed by a self-directed rebuild onto Azure, Snowflake, dbt Cloud and BigQuery.

My Journey

I started my career at Infosys, progressing through four promotions over four years while working across three global banking clients. I began by supporting the data migration workstream for the three-way merger that formed Bank of Baroda — profiling legacy schemas and writing PL/SQL transformation scripts across 50M+ customer accounts. From there I moved into building SQL-based ETL routines at ING, and went on to own the reporting layer end-to-end at ANZ, writing T-SQL, views and stored procedures and delivering Power BI dashboards used directly by business stakeholders.

In July 2022, I relocated from India to the UK and took a career break to focus on family relocation and caregiving. Rather than let my technical skills stall, I used that time deliberately: I completed the Google Data Analytics and Google Advanced Data Analytics Professional Certificates, and then designed and built four production-style data projects from scratch, spanning Azure, Snowflake, dbt Cloud, BigQuery, Prefect and Power BI — the same modern stack most UK data teams are hiring for.

Today I'm looking for a Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer or Data Analyst role in the UK where I can bring that combination of banking-grade rigour around data quality and governance, and genuine hands-on comfort with a modern cloud data platform.
